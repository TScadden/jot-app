How Tabs works.

Log your health, spot your patterns, and share clear reports with your doctor.

The mechanism

From daily logs to doctor-ready reports.

Summaries and insights are generated with the help of Google's Gemini API. You review every insight before it goes anywhere — nothing is shared without you.

Privacy, in detail

The full story, no fine print.

Everything below comes straight from our privacy policy — the short version, in plain language.

What's collected

  • Your logs, notes, and documents you choose to upload.
  • Health data from Google Health Connect — heart rate, sleep, activity, nutrition — only if you grant permission.
  • Location only to fetch weather context for your entries. We don't store your location history.

Where it lives

Your health data is synchronized and stored on our secure servers so it works across your devices and can power your insights. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS; on your phone, Android's Keystore protects local keys.

Who processes it

Google's Gemini API acts as our subprocessor to generate your summaries and insights. Processing follows Google's Gemini API terms — see the privacy policy for the details on data use.

How to delete everything

Delete your account in the app's Settings and your data is purged from our active databases. No app installed? Use the web deletion portal or email support — manual requests are processed within 48 hours.

What we will never claim: HIPAA certification, or that your data never leaves your device — it syncs to our servers and to Gemini, and we'd rather tell you that plainly. Read the full policy

For your doctor

Walk in with the summary they wish every patient brought.

What the report contains. Key patterns across your logs, a symptom log summary, biometrics from Health Connect, your medications, and protocol adherence — one clean PDF per visit.

Protocol sync, explained. Upload your doctor's instructions once. Tabs tracks your daily data against your prescribed protocols and folds adherence into the report — so "are you following the plan?" has an actual answer.

Bring this to your appointment. Instead of trying to remember three months of symptoms in a fifteen-minute visit, hand over the summary and spend the time on decisions.
