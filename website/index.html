Privacy

Your health data belongs to you.

Encrypted in transit

TLS encryption between your phone and our servers.

Never sold

We do not sell your health data, and we never use it for advertising.

Delete anytime

Delete your account in Settings and your data is purged.

No location tracking

Location is used only for weather context on your logs. We don't store your location history.

Your logs sync to our secure servers so they work across your devices. Summaries and insights are generated with the help of Google's Gemini API. Read the full story in our privacy policy
