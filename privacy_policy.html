Privacy Policy for Tabs

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Welcome to Tabs. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how Tabs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App") and associated backend services.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (securely hashed) to authenticate your access, secure your data, and sync it across your devices.

b. Health and Wellness Data (Google Health Connect)

If you explicitly grant permissions, Tabs accesses wellness and health data from Google Health Connect (such as heart rate, sleep history, activity logs, and nutrition). This health data is synchronized and stored on our backend servers to enable cross-device sync and power our AI-driven wellness insights. We do not sell this data to third parties, nor do we use it for advertising.

c. User-Generated Content

We collect the logs, categories, and custom notes you write within the App to provide the core logging and notepad functions.

d. Files and Documents

If you upload PDFs, text files, or other documents to the "Knowledge Base," these files are transmitted to and stored on our servers to extract context for your personal AI model.

e. Device Permissions and Usage

The App requests access to specific device features to enable core functions:

  • Location (Fine/Coarse): Used to retrieve local weather context for your journal entries. Location data is processed locally on your device and sent to the weather API. We do not track or store your persistent location history.
  • Calendar (Read/Write): Used to display and sync your schedule context with your log notes.
  • Microphone / Record Audio: Used to record audio notes for the "Voice AI Log" feature. Audio is recorded only when you explicitly press the record button and is processed to transcribe your voice notes.
  • Bluetooth and Connected Devices: Used to scan for and connect to external wellness devices (such as heart rate sensors).
  • Notifications: Used to send you alerts for habits, reminders, and heart-rate spikes.

2. How We Use and Process Information (including AI)

We use your information, including health metrics and uploaded documents, to provide personal health summaries and AI-driven insights. Specifically, your data (including health details and document text) is transmitted to the Google Gemini API for processing to generate summaries of your logs and notes. This processing is subject to Google's Gemini API Terms of Service. Under paid API configurations, Google does not use your content to train Google models; however, free-tier API usage may allow Google to use inputs for model improvement. You should review Google's Gemini API privacy documentation for details on your active deployment's data usage.

3. Data Sharing and Recipients

We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Play Services: For secure user authentication and payment processing.
  • Google Gemini API: As a subprocessor for running text summaries and health insight models.
  • Database Host: Secure cloud databases hosted in cloud server environments.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information, user-generated logs, and documents as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, which will initiate the deletion of your records from our active databases.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • In-App Deletion: Delete your account and purge all associated personal, log, and health data instantly via the App settings.
  • External Account Deletion Method: If you do not have the App installed or prefer an external method, you can request full account and data deletion by visiting our web portal at jottracker.com/delete-account.html or by sending an email request to our support team at support@notel.com. Manual requests are processed within 48 hours.
  • Revoke Permissions: Disconnect Google Health Connect permissions at any time via your Android system settings.

6. Security Practices

We implement security measures to protect your data, including encrypting data in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) and utilizing secure server configurations. Locally, Android key storage is secured using the Android Keystore System.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@notel.com.
