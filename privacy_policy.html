Privacy Policy for Tabs

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Welcome to Tabs. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how Tabs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App") and associated backend services.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (securely hashed) to authenticate your access, secure your data, and sync it across your devices.

b. Health and Wellness Data (Google Health Connect)

If you explicitly grant permissions, Tabs accesses wellness and health data from Google Health Connect (such as heart rate, sleep history, activity logs, and nutrition). Contrary to local-only use, **this health data is synchronized and stored securely on our backend servers** to enable cross-device sync and power our AI-driven wellness insights. We do not sell this data to third parties, nor do we use it for advertising.

c. User-Generated Content

We collect the logs, categories, and custom notes you write within the App to provide the core logging and notepad functions.

d. Files and Documents

If you upload PDFs, text files, or other documents to the "Knowledge Base," these files are transmitted to and stored on our servers to extract context for your personal AI model.

2. How We Use and Process Information (including AI)

We use your information, including health metrics and uploaded documents, to provide personal health summaries and AI-driven insights. Specifically, **your data (including health details and document text) is transmitted to the Google Gemini API** for processing. Google Gemini processes this data via a secure server-side connection to generate your health summaries and summaries of your notes. Under our API terms, Google Gemini is a subprocessor and is prohibited from using your data to train or refine its foundational AI models.

3. Data Sharing and Recipients

We share data only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Play Services: For secure user authentication and payment processing.
  • Google Gemini API: As a secure subprocessor for running text summaries and health insight models.
  • Database Host: Secure cloud databases hosted in encrypted AWS environments.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information, user-generated logs, and documents as long as your account remains active. Your raw synchronized Health Connect data is stored on our servers and retained for a maximum of 90 days, after which it is deleted or aggregated into non-identifiable statistics, unless you delete your account sooner.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • In-App Deletion: Delete your account and purge all associated personal, log, and health data instantly via the App settings.
  • External Account Deletion Method: If you do not have the App installed or prefer an external method, you can request full account and data deletion by visiting our web portal at jottracker.com/delete-account.html or by sending an email request to our support team at support@jottracker.com. All requests are processed within 48 hours.
  • Revoke Permissions: Disconnect Google Health Connect permissions at any time via your Android system settings.

6. Security Practices

We implement robust industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Data is encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Android keystores are secured locally using Android Keystore System hardware-backed encryption.
