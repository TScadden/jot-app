Privacy Policy for Tabs

Effective Date: March 9, 2026

Welcome to Tabs. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how Tabs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password to secure your data and sync it across your devices.

b. Health Data (Health Connect)

If you grant permission, Tabs accesses data from Google Health Connect (such as heart rate, sleep, activity, and nutrition). This data is used locally on your device to provide insights and recaps. We do not sell this data to third parties.

c. User-Generated Content

We collect the notes, logs, and categories you create within the app to provide the core notepad and AI analysis functionality.

d. Files and Documents

If you upload PDFs or text files to the "Knowledge Base," these files are processed to extract information for your personal AI model.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the service.
  • To generate AI insights, summaries, and weekly recaps.
  • To synchronize your data across multiple devices.
  • To process in-app purchases and manage your credit balance.

3. Data Security and Storage

Your data is stored securely on our encrypted servers. AI processing is performed via secure proxies, and we do not store your raw health data longer than necessary to generate requested insights.

4. Third-Party Services

Tabs utilizes Google Play Services for authentication and billing. Our AI features utilize large language model providers (like Google Gemini) via a secure server-side proxy to protect your identifying information.

5. Your Choices

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings. You can also revoke Health Connect permissions through your Android system settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@notel.com.
